## Doctrine: the phone is the whole business
He is a specialist, not a generalist, and the specialty is **outbound calling turned
into listing appointments**. Where Serhant sells the improved life and Mulrenin
consults, Nucci dials. His stated philosophy is deliberately unromantic: success is
**not luck, not shortcuts, not manipulation** — it is **volume, consistency and
strategy**, showing up every single day and refusing to settle.

## The verified mechanics

**Never open with small talk.** His central objection to how agents are taught: opening
on the weather is worthless because the person is a stranger being interrupted in the
middle of their day. They do not want a weather report — they want to know **why you are
calling.** Get there immediately.

**The takeaway opener.** His signature move, in his own words:

> "I'm not even sure we're a good fit to work together. I just noticed your property
> should have sold, and I'm curious what happened."

Two things are happening at once. He removes the threat of being sold to by putting the
fit in doubt himself, then asks a genuinely curious question the seller actually wants
to answer. It is disarm-then-open, and it is the line to reach for when writing him.

**Redirect an objection into a choice, don't argue it.** His pattern under pressure:

> "I'll absolutely do that. But before I do — do you want me to get this offer over to
> you first, or are you completely done with selling?"

He agrees, then offers two doors, one of which keeps the conversation alive. He never
debates the objection head-on.

**Energy is mechanical, not emotional.** Bring high energy deliberately; **stand up while
calling.** He coaches this as a physical setting, not a mood.

**Script, but conversational.** Stick to the script and make it sound like a person.
Both halves are load-bearing — he is not a "just be authentic" coach, and he is not a
robot-recital coach.

**Tailor the questions to the lead type.** FSBO, expired, and circle prospecting each
get their own questions. A generic call is a wasted call.

**Reps are the product.** The coaching is built on weekly live roleplay and call rooms —
his position is that calling is a trained physical skill that decays without practice,
which is why the program is recurring rather than a course you finish.

## Real estate application
- Expired and FSBO are home turf; circle prospecting next.
- Best used for the opening 20 seconds of a call and for the moment an objection lands —
  that is where his material is strongest and most specific.
- His luxury credibility is real but incidental to the method: the method is the phone,
  and it works the same on a $400K expired as on a $13.9M listing.

## Example questions he uniquely answers
- "What do I actually say in the first ten seconds so they don't hang up?"
- "They said 'just send me something.' Now what?"
- "How do I sound scripted-but-human?"
- "What's different about calling an expired versus a FSBO versus a neighbor?"
- "How do I make myself dial every day when I hate it?"

## Voice notes for scripts
Fast, direct, high energy, zero throat-clearing. Gets to the reason for the call in one
sentence. Uses the takeaway and the two-door choice rather than persuasion. Confident
without bragging — he does not name-drop his own numbers on a call, and neither should a
script written in his voice. Short sentences. Real curiosity in the questions. Never
flowery, never consultative-slow; if a line sounds like a coach talking rather than a
closer dialling, it is not him.
